Project updates

Progress deserves to be visible.

A place for thoughtful updates on milestones, decisions, verified contributions, and lessons along the way.

Launch note · November 2026
The website is being established as the public home for The Stability Initiative. The project is in its initiation and planning stage. The funding target and allocations remain provisional.

What will be shared here

  • Milestones reached and next steps.
  • Updates to the needs assessment and project plan.
  • Verified contribution and expenditure summaries, when available.
  • Community resources and opportunities added to the initiative.
  • Changes to the plan and the reasons behind them.

Our commitment to transparency

Updates will distinguish between amounts pledged, amounts received, and amounts spent. Paid service revenue, business revenue, barter value, and community contributions will be tracked separately rather than combined into one fundraising total.

Personal account information, private donor details, addresses, and sensitive documents will not be published.

This page will be updated as verified information becomes available. No impact figures or testimonials will be invented to make the project appear further along than it is.
