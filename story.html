My story

This is not a last-minute request. This is a long-awaited step forward.

Behind this initiative is a person who has continued working, planning, learning, and looking for a way forward—even through a difficult season.

For the past year, I have been living in my vehicle. That is a reality I am not sharing for sympathy or to make hardship the center of my identity. I am sharing it because it is part of the circumstances that make stability an urgent and meaningful goal.

What brought me here

Reaching this point has taken time. I have been working to organize my priorities, understand what is needed, and create a practical plan instead of making an unstructured appeal. This initiative represents a deliberate step toward turning that preparation into action.

I am seeking a path toward independent housing, reliable transportation, essential resources, and a stronger foundation for income through self-employment and business development.

What I am building toward

I want stability to create room for more than simply getting through the day. I want it to make it possible to keep developing ideas, contribute my skills, build sustainable work, and create opportunities for others to connect with resources and one another.

I know what it means to need support. I also know that needing support does not erase what a person can contribute. That belief is part of why The Stability Initiative includes a community resource exchange—not only a way to receive help, but a place where people can offer what they have to give.

My intention: To approach this next chapter with transparency, responsibility, and a commitment to making meaningful progress—while creating something that can serve a wider community.

Thank you for taking the time to understand the story and the purpose behind this work.
