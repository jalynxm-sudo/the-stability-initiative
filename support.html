What support is intended to help with

The provisional $12,000 target is intended to support the following priorities:

  • Housing and move-in requirements: $5,000
  • Food, clothing, and necessities: $1,500
  • Emergency savings: $1,500
  • Vehicle principal reduction: $4,000

These allocations are provisional and subject to verification as actual costs are confirmed.

Other ways to help

  • Share a housing, employment, or business opportunity.
  • Offer a practical resource or professional introduction.
  • Explore a paid service or mutually agreed skill exchange.
  • Share the initiative with someone who may be interested in its purpose.
