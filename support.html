Give support

A contribution can help create room to move forward.

Financial support can help address immediate stability priorities while this initiative continues to develop its longer-term plan.

Transparency: The Stability Initiative is an individual-led community initiative, not a registered charitable organization. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.

What support is intended to help with

The provisional $12,000 target is intended to support housing and move-in requirements, essential necessities, emergency savings, and a planned vehicle principal reduction. The allocation is subject to verification and may change as actual costs are confirmed.

Choose how you would like to participate

If you are interested in making a financial contribution, please use a verified payment link once one has been established. The payment link is not yet connected on this launch version, so no payment should be sent through this page.

Other ways to help

  • Share a housing, employment, or business opportunity.
  • Offer a practical resource or professional introduction.
  • Explore a paid service or mutually agreed skill exchange.
  • Share the initiative with someone who may be interested in its purpose.

Updates will distinguish financial contributions from service revenue, business revenue, and non-cash exchanges. Progress figures will only be published when supported by actual records.
