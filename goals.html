The plan

A practical foundation for what comes next.

The initiative is organized around defined needs, a provisional funding target, and a commitment to track progress responsibly.

Planning note: The $12,000 target and allocations below are provisional planning figures. They are not a guarantee that all needs or local housing costs will be covered. Amounts may be revised as costs are verified.

How progress will be approached

  • Verify costs and requirements before committing funds.
  • Prioritize safe, sustainable housing and essential needs.
  • Keep records of contributions and expenditures.
  • Share periodic updates without exposing private financial or identifying information.
  • Adjust the plan when verified information shows a change is necessary.

The initiative also includes a longer-term income strategy centered on self-employment and business development. Stability is the immediate priority; sustainable income is part of the foundation for maintaining it.
